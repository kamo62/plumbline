# Code of conduct

We want people to contribute without harassment or intimidation. Be respectful,
constructive and considerate in issues, reviews and other project spaces. Critique
ideas and work without attacking people. Respect privacy and consent; do not publish
someone else's private information or make unwanted sexual, discriminatory,
threatening or abusive remarks.

Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict participation
when behaviour breaches this policy. Decisions should be proportionate to the
behaviour and protect those affected. Do not retaliate against someone who reports
or participates in a report.

If project content or a participant breaches this policy, use GitHub's reporting
controls for the relevant content or account. Avoid posting private details in a
public issue. GitHub handles reports made through its own controls under its policies.
